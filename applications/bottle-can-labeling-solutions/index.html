Bottle & Can Labeling Solutions

The PLB700 is a high-speed automatic round bottle labeling machine that uses a starwheel bottle-spacing and positioning system to separate containers and guide them through the labeling station. It applies self-adhesive labels by automatic wrap-around labeling to round bottles and jars, at 60–100 bottles/min with ±1.0 mm labeling accuracy, for bottle diameters of Φ30–100 mm.

How to Choose

Labeling quality follows the container and the label. These are the factors that decide the setup for a round bottle labeling line.

Container diameter — suitable bottle diameter is Φ30–100 mm; confirm the final setup by actual bottle shape and dimensions. Label size — suitability depends on container diameter, height and label size; actual speed and accuracy also depend on label dimensions and label material. Label position — the starwheel positions each container as it moves through the labeling station, helping keep wrap-around label placement consistent. Line speed — standard labeling capacity is approximately 60–100 bottles/min; actual speed depends on bottle diameter, bottle height, label dimensions, label material, conveyor speed and product stability. Bottle spacing — the starwheel separates containers and guides them through the labeling station, providing more controlled bottle spacing at higher speeds than a conventional bottle stop. Printing integration — tell us whether printing integration is needed for your line, so the configuration can be confirmed for your application.
