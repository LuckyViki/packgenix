About Packgenix

Packgenix Tech Co., Ltd., established in 2008, is a packaging machinery manufacturer based in Jiangsu and Guangdong, China. We supply filling, labeling, premade pouch, vacuum and thermoforming equipment to international B2B customers, with OEM/ODM support.
