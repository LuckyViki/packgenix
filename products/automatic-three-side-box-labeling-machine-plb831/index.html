Product Introduction

The PLB831 Automatic Three-Side Box Labeling Machine is designed for applying self-adhesive labels across three connected surfaces of small boxes and rectangular packages.

Instead of placing a label on only one flat surface, the machine guides the sticker across the package and applies it continuously over multiple sides. The machine combines automatic product detection, label dispensing, multi-surface application and conveyor transport in one continuous process.

Applications & Sizes

The final machine setup should be confirmed according to the package dimensions, surface shape and required label position. Standard product size: 40–400 mm long × 40–200 mm wide × 0.5–150 mm high.

Standard label size: 6–300 mm long × 15–130 mm wide. The final label dimensions should be confirmed together with the product size and required three-side wrapping position.

Key Features

01

Three-Side Label Application

The PLB831 applies one label continuously across three surfaces of a package, giving a more secure and integrated appearance than single-surface labeling. The sticker can also serve as product identification, tamper-evident closure, decoration, promotion or package sealing support.

02

Adjustable Top Labeling Structure

The height and position of the upper labeling mechanism can be adjusted according to the size and shape of the product. This makes it easier to adapt the machine to different box dimensions and label positions.

03

Label Smoothing Brush

A brush in the labeling section presses the sticker evenly onto the package. This helps improve label adhesion, reduce trapped air and bubbles, and improve the appearance of the finished package.

04

Touchscreen Control

The machine is equipped with a touchscreen control panel. Operators can adjust labeling speed, timing and production settings, and labeling positions can be adjusted when changing package sizes. The operating language can also be customized according to customer requirements.

05

Label Detection Sensor

The label sensor identifies the gap between labels according to light transmission characteristics. This allows the machine to detect and separate labels automatically, improving labeling consistency and positioning accuracy.

06

Automatic Product Detection

An electric-eye sensor detects products as they pass through the conveyor, and the control system activates the labeling sequence automatically, synchronizing label dispensing with product movement. Automatic detection, dispensing and transport reduce repetitive manual operation.

07

Adjustable Conveyor Guides

The width of the conveyor guard rails can be adjusted according to product dimensions. Proper guide adjustment helps keep products aligned during transport and improves label application stability. The conveyor width can also be customized.

08

±1 mm Labeling Accuracy

The standard labeling accuracy is approximately ±1 mm. Actual accuracy depends on package consistency, label quality, conveyor alignment, product positioning and machine setup.

How It Works

Products are placed on the conveyor and automatically transported toward the labeling station. An electric-eye sensor detects each incoming package and sends a signal to the control system.

The label sensor identifies the position of the self-adhesive label on the backing paper. The label is then dispensed and applied to the product through the customized labeling structure.

Key Machine Details

The main difference between the PLB831 and a conventional flat labeling machine is the customized three-surface application structure. The label is first dispensed onto the package and then progressively pressed across adjacent surfaces.

Working speed is approximately 15–20 pcs/min and labeling accuracy is approximately ±1 mm. Maximum label roll outer diameter Φ300 mm, inner diameter Φ76 mm. Actual output depends on product dimensions, label length, number of surfaces covered, conveyor speed, label material and product spacing.
